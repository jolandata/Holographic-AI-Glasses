# Holographic AI Glasses

**Advanced Wearable Computing | Holographic Interfaces | Human-Centred AI**

An interdisciplinary research and development initiative exploring next-generation wearable holographic AI systems, integrating spatial computing and human-centred interaction.

The project brings together expertise in optical engineering, wearable technologies, artificial intelligence and human–computer interaction.

## Collaboration Opportunities

We welcome enquiries from optical engineers, glass and photonics specialists, product-development engineers, research institutions and industry partners interested in collaborative research and development.

Technical documentation, engineering specifications and research findings are maintained privately to protect ongoing research and intellectual property.

**Project status:** Research and engineering feasibility development.
